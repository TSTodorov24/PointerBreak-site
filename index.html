THE GAME

Start in the top-left corner, reach the green exit in the bottom-right corner. A new maze is built every time.

🧱

Random mazes

Recursive Backtracker builds a new maze every game, from 10×10 to 50×50. A way out always exists.

🚪

Shortcut doors

Blinking orange walls you can walk through, placed only where they really save steps.

🎥

Smooth camera

Follows your monster and zooms out to the whole maze while you hold M.

💡

One hint

The shortest way to the exit for 3 seconds. Once per game, and it costs 20% of your score.

🏆

High scores

Top 5 for every difficulty, saved in a file, with 3-letter arcade names.

🔤

Text view

Press T and the maze turns into characters: # wall, D door, @ you, E exit.

HOW TO PLAY

You cannot walk through walls, except through the doors. Beat the clock on Medium and Hard.

UNDER THE HOOD

The game logic never touches raylib or cin/cout, so 48 automatic tests can check it without opening a window.

Build

Every cell is one int with bits for walls and doors. Memory is freed in the destructor.

int** grid = new int*[size];

Carve

Recursive Backtracker walks, removes walls and steps back at dead ends.

stack → neighbour → remove wall

Shortcuts

A door appears only if walking around its wall takes at least 10 steps.

if (steps >= 10) placeDoor();

Solve

The same BFS gives the hint, the time limit and the score.

FindShortestPath(grid, …)
